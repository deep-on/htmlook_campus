## Account

**How do I change my email?**
Settings → Account → Email. We'll send a confirmation link to the new address;
the change takes effect once you click it.

**Can I have more than one workspace?**
Yes. Free accounts get one workspace; paid plans get unlimited. Switch with the
workspace picker in the top-left.

**How do I delete my account?**
Settings → Account → Delete account. This is permanent and removes all notes
after a 7-day grace period.

## Billing

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Settings → Billing → Cancel plan. You keep paid features until the end of the
current period, then drop to the free tier — your notes are never deleted.

**Do you offer refunds?**
We refund annual plans within 30 days of purchase, no questions asked. Monthly
plans aren't pro-rated, but you can cancel any time.

**Can I switch from monthly to annual?**
Yes — Settings → Billing → Change plan. We credit the unused portion of your
monthly charge toward the annual price.

## Syncing

**Why aren't my notes syncing?**
Check that you're online and signed in to the same account on both devices.
Pull-to-refresh forces a sync. If a note still won't appear, see
[Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting).

**Does Bramble work offline?**
Yes. Edits are saved locally and sync automatically when you reconnect.

## Privacy

**Are my notes encrypted?**
Notes are encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. End-to-end encryption is
available on the Team plan.

**Do you train models on my notes?**
No. Your content is never used for model training.

## Troubleshooting

**I'm seeing a 401 error.**
Your session expired — sign out and back in. If it persists, clear the app
cache (Settings → Advanced → Clear cache).

**Search isn't finding a note I know exists.**
Search indexes in the background after a sync. Give it a minute, or reindex
from Settings → Advanced → Rebuild search.

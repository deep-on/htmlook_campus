## Billing

**How do I cancel?**
Settings → Billing → Cancel plan. You keep paid features until the period ends.

**Can I get a refund?**
Annual plans are refundable within 30 days of purchase.

## Technical

**Why am I seeing a 401?**
Your session expired — sign out and back in to refresh it.

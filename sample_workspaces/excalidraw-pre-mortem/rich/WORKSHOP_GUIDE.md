# Pre-mortem Workshop Guide (90 min) · Vela Insights

Premise: start from *"It's Q4. The Vela Insights launch failed. Why?"* and work
backwards.

## Agenda
1. **Opening (10 min)** — explain the goal and scenario. Make clear this is a
   what-if exercise, not a blame session.
2. **Silent brainstorm (15 min)** — everyone writes failure causes on sticky
   notes, in silence.
3. **Affinity grouping (20 min)** — cluster similar risks into five big
   hypotheses (onboarding / pricing / latency / partners / import).
4. **Dot voting (10 min)** — three dots each; pick the most worrying risks.
5. **Mitigation (25 min)** — assign an owner and one mitigation action per risk.
   Fill in the green cards on the board.
6. **Synthesis (10 min)** — plot dots on the risk matrix to lock priorities and
   agree on next actions.

## diagrams/
- `pre-mortem-board.excalidraw` — main board (failure hypotheses + mitigation +
  owner)
- `risk-matrix.excalidraw` — likelihood × impact matrix

## Outputs
- Owners pull the top-right (high-likelihood, high-impact) risks into the next
  sprint as actions.
- Export the board to SVG and attach it to the launch readiness doc.

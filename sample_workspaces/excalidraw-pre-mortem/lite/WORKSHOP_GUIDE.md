# Pre-mortem Workshop Guide (90 min) · Vela Insights

## Agenda
1. **Opening (10 min)** — goal / scenario ("It's Q4. The launch failed. Why?")
2. **Silent brainstorm (15 min)** — everyone writes sticky notes
3. **Affinity grouping (20 min)** — cluster similar risks
4. **Dot voting (10 min)** — pick the most worrying risks
5. **Mitigation (25 min)** — owner + mitigation action per risk
6. **Synthesis (10 min)** — wrap up + next actions

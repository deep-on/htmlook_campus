# What's New — v1.0.18

Released: **2026-06-13** · Download: [htmlook.app](https://htmlook.app)

> Other languages: [한국어](Whats-New-v1.0.18-ko.md)

Two big themes this version: a full toolkit for **making and refining
video**, and a viewer you can **split into several panes** to read and
edit documents side by side.

---

## Make video

Give it a short description or a photo and it turns into a video. You
connect the key for whichever video-generation service you already use,
so you can pick the one you like. It also renders code-defined motion
graphics to video.

## Edit video

Trim out what you don't need and stitch the rest, drop captions and
titles anywhere on the frame, and add slow zoom, speed changes, and
fades. Long silences between words can be removed automatically.

## Captions & voice

- **Auto captions** — generate captions for a video that has none, and
  translate them into another language.
- **AI narration** — read text aloud over the video, optionally in your
  own voice after a short training step.
- **Speaker labeling** — automatically tell apart who's speaking in a
  video with several people.

## Several documents on one screen

Split the viewer into **2, 3, or 4 panes** (side by side · stacked ·
2×2 grid) to see different documents at once. Each pane holds its own
file and zoom level; drag the dividers to resize, and turn on the lock
so scrolling one pane moves the others. The split layout is remembered
per workspace.

## Edit the source · scroll to zoom

- Edit **Markdown · HTML · text** right in **Source** view — it used to
  be read-only.
- In both the document viewer and the terminal, **Cmd+scroll** (or a
  trackpad pinch) zooms in and out.

## Tools, in one place

Install the external tools the video features need from the **Tool
Center** in one go, and check what's missing or broken. The status bar
tells you right away when something is missing.

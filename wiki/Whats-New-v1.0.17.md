# What's New — v1.0.17

Released: **2026-05-31** · Download: [htmlook.app](https://htmlook.app)

> Other languages: [한국어](Whats-New-v1.0.17-ko.md)

Two big themes this cycle: a refined way to start a new workspace from an
example, and the whole app in four languages. A round of settings and
terminal polish sits on top.

---

## Examples: simple, or rich

When you pick an example in the **New Workspace** wizard, you can now
choose **Simple** — a minimal scaffold to start fast — or **Rich** — a
finished example you can read and reference right away. Around 40
examples (slide decks, diagrams, data notebooks, docs) ship at both
depths.

## Examples you can actually reference

Slide decks, architecture diagrams, data-analysis notebooks, and API
docs were rewritten to a real-world level. The empty whiteboard and
mind-map examples are now filled with actual drawings.

## Four languages, menu bar included

Pick **Korean · English · 日本語 · 中文** in **Settings → General →
Language**. The New Workspace wizard, the settings screens, AI settings,
and even the top menu bar all switch to your chosen language.

## Daily polish

- The **General** settings tab is grouped into *General · Window ·
  Appearance*, and the **Terminal** tab into clear sections — easier to
  find what you need. Decorative background patterns tuck under a
  *Decorative* toggle.
- The **New Workspace** wizard now visually separates picking a profile
  from picking an example, and the example step is calmer.
- The shortcut hint that appears over the terminal has a **close
  button** (turn it off for good with "Don't show again"), and it no
  longer disappears when you move the mouse — so it's easy to dismiss.
